# DISC Behavioral Style Assessment

## Test Description
The DISC Behavioral Style Assessment is based on William Moulton Marston's behavioral model, measuring your style across four dimensions:
- **D (Dominance)** — Direct, decisive, results-oriented, competitive
- **I (Influence)** — Outgoing, enthusiastic, sociable, optimistic
- **S (Steadiness)** — Patient, calm, supportive, team-oriented
- **C (Conscientiousness)** — Precise, analytical, systematic, quality-focused

- D. Move forward steadily, ensuring the decision is sound and reliable *[S+2, C+1]*

## Scoring Guide

Each option is marked with the corresponding behavioral style score:
- **D** = Dominance — Direct, decisive, results-oriented
- **I** = Influence — Outgoing, enthusiastic, sociable
- **S** = Steadiness — Patient, calm, stability-seeking
- **C** = Conscientiousness — Precise, analytical, logic-focused

The number indicates the weight of that option on the corresponding dimension. Your DISC behavioral style is determined by the total scores across all dimensions.
