# Sexual Orientation Spectrum Assessment

## Test Description
Sexual orientation exists on a diverse spectrum rather than a simple binary. This assessment, inspired by the Klein Sexual Orientation Grid, helps you understand your orientation across multiple dimensions:
- **Opposite-Sex Attraction** — Emotional and sexual attraction to people of a different gender
- **Same-Sex Attraction** — Emotional and sexual attraction to people of the same gender
- **Emotional Bonding** — Gender preferences when forming deep emotional connections
- **Self-Identification** — Your subjective understanding and positioning of your own orientation

This assessment does not label or judge. It is designed to help you better understand yourself.

## Scoring Guide

This assessment evaluates across four dimensions:
- **异性吸引 (Opposite-Sex Attraction)** — Emotional and sexual attraction to people of a different gender
- **同性吸引 (Same-Sex Attraction)** — Emotional and sexual attraction to people of the same gender
- **情感联结 (Emotional Bonding)** — Capacity for deep emotional connection beyond gender
- **自我认同 (Self-Identification)** — Clarity and acceptance of one's own sexual orientation

The result is presented as a spectrum, helping you understand your position within the diverse landscape of sexual orientation. Remember: sexual orientation is personal, diverse, and deserving of respect.
