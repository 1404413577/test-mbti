# Resilience Assessment

## Test Description
The Resilience Assessment measures your ability to bounce back from adversity across four core dimensions:
- **Emotional Regulation** — Ability to manage negative emotions and maintain inner balance
- **Optimism** — Capacity to maintain positive expectations and see hope in difficult situations
- **Self-Efficacy** — Confidence in your ability to solve problems and handle challenges
- **Social Support** — Ability to build and maintain supportive interpersonal relationships

## Questions

- C. It takes quite a while; I sometimes ruminate on it *[情绪调节+1]*
- D. It's hard to recover on my own; I need external support *[社会支持+2]*

## Scoring Guide

Each option is marked with the corresponding resilience dimension score:
- **情绪调节** (Emotional Regulation) — Ability to manage negative emotions and maintain inner balance
- **乐观主义** (Optimism) — Capacity to maintain positive expectations and see hope in difficulties
- **自我效能** (Self-Efficacy) — Confidence in your problem-solving and challenge-handling abilities
- **社会支持** (Social Support) — Ability to build and maintain supportive relationships

The number indicates the weight of that option on the corresponding dimension. Your resilience level is determined by the total scores across all dimensions.
