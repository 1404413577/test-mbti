# EQ Test (Emotional Intelligence)

## Test Description
Emotional intelligence refers to the ability to recognize, understand, and manage your own emotions, as well as perceive, understand, and positively influence the emotions of others. This assessment covers four core dimensions:
- **Self-Awareness** – understanding your own emotions
- **Self-Management** – regulating and controlling emotions effectively
- **Social Awareness** – perceiving and understanding others' emotions
- **Relationship Management** – building and maintaining healthy relationships
