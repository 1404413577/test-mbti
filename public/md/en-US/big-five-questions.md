# Big Five Personality Test (OCEAN)

## Test Description
This test is based on the Big Five model (OCEAN). It evaluates five core personality dimensions through situational questions:
- **O Openness** (Curiosity, imagination, aesthetic appreciation)
- **C Conscientiousness** (Self-discipline, organization, responsibility)
- **E Extraversion** (Sociability, activity, assertiveness)
- **A Agreeableness** (Empathy, cooperation, trust)
- **N Emotional Stability** (Calmness and resilience - reverse of Neuroticism)

## Questions

### 1. Attitude to new things (Openness)
**Question**: When you face a brand-new idea or activity, you usually…
- A. Can't wait to explore and try it out *[O+3]*
- B. Interested but prefer to learn more before trying *[O+2]*
- C. It depends; sometimes I try *[O+1]*
- D. Prefer to keep the status quo, avoid changes *[O+0]*

### 2. Planning and execution (Conscientiousness)
**Question**: For a moderately complex task, you usually…
- A. Make a detailed plan and follow it strictly *[C+3]*
- B. Set a rough plan and adjust flexibly *[C+2]*
- C. Start doing and figure it out on the go *[C+1]*
- D. Procrastinate or get easily distracted *[C+0]*

### 3. Social preference (Extraversion)
**Question**: A weekend party for you is…
- A. An energy source; love interacting with people *[E+3]*
- B. A fine option depending on mood *[E+2]*
- C. Occasional participation is enough *[E+1]*
- D. Prefer solitude to recharge *[E+0]*

### 4. Cooperation and empathy (Agreeableness)
**Question**: When your team has disagreements, you usually…
- A. Proactively listen and seek common ground *[A+3]*
- B. Understand others but hold on key points *[A+2]*
- C. Mainly defend your own standpoint *[A+1]*
- D. Speak bluntly, easily ignore others' feelings *[A+0]*

### 5. Response under stress (Emotional Stability)
**Question**: When the deadline is close and pressure is high, you…
- A. Stay calm and progress step by step *[N+3]*
- B. Feel tense but still in control *[N+2]*
- C. Noticeable anxiety, need external push *[N+1]*
- D. Emotional and efficiency drops significantly *[N+0]*

### 6. Aesthetics and imagination (Openness)
**Question**: Your view on art/aesthetics/imagination?
- A. Highly value it, often immersed *[O+3]*
- B. Enjoy and appreciate *[O+2]*
- C. So-so *[O+1]*
- D. Don't care much *[O+0]*

### 7. Self-discipline habits (Conscientiousness)
**Question**: For long-term goals (fitness/study), you…
- A. Can steadily maintain and track progress *[C+3]*
- B. Most of the time can keep it *[C+2]*
- C. On and off *[C+1]*
- D. Hard to persist *[C+0]*

### 8. Active expression (Extraversion)
**Question**: When expressing opinions in a group, you…
- A. Happy to speak and lead discussion *[E+3]*
- B. Speak when appropriate *[E+2]*
- C. Occasionally express *[E+1]*
- D. Mostly silent *[E+0]*

### 9. Trust and cooperation (Agreeableness)
**Question**: Your intuitive judgment of others' motives?
- A. Mostly trustworthy and willing to cooperate *[A+3]*
- B. Cautiously optimistic *[A+2]*
- C. Reserved attitude *[A+1]*
- D. Tend to doubt *[A+0]*

### 10. Emotional recovery (Emotional Stability)
**Question**: After setbacks, you recover to stable state in…
- A. Very short time *[N+3]*
- B. Some time needed *[N+2]*
- C. Relatively long time *[N+1]*
- D. Very hard to self-recover *[N+0]*

## Scoring
Each option contributes points to dimensions shown in brackets (e.g., O+2 means +2 to Openness). Calculate totals across O/C/E/A/N to determine your profile.
