# MBTI Personality Test

## Test Description
The MBTI (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator) is based on Jung's psychological type theory. It identifies preferences across four dichotomies:
- **Extraversion (E) vs Introversion (I)** – where you gain energy and direct attention
- **Sensing (S) vs Intuition (N)** – how you collect and process information
- **Thinking (T) vs Feeling (F)** – how you make decisions
- **Judging (J) vs Perceiving (P)** – how you organize your life and deal with the outer world
